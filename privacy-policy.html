Leave By

Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 19, 2026

Leave By ("the app," "we," "our") is a personal travel-time alert app. This policy explains what information the app handles and how, as of this build.

Summary: Leave By stores your events and preferences in a private cloud account so your leave-by alerts can reach you even when the app is closed. Your data is visible only to you, is used only to run the app's features, and is never sold, used for advertising, or shared with anyone beyond the two service providers that make the app work (Google Firebase and Mapbox). The app contains no analytics and no trackers.

Information the app stores in your account

When you first open the app, an anonymous account is created for you automatically — no sign-up step, no email required. The following is stored in that account, in Google's Firebase Cloud Firestore (servers located in the United States), protected by security rules so only your signed-in device can read or write it:

  • Event details: titles, locations (the address text you type, plus the map coordinates it resolves to), start/end times, event type, transport mode, per-event buffer adjustments, and cancelled status.
  • Computed alert schedule: the times the app has calculated to notify you for each event, stored server-side so alerts fire on time even when the app is closed.
  • Push notification tokens: the device token needed to deliver notifications to your phone (this identifies the app install, not you personally).
  • App preferences: default buffer, enabled transport modes and alert stages, and theme are stored on your device.

Optional: Google Sign-In and Google Calendar

If you connect Google Calendar in Settings (entirely optional):

  • Your Google account's basic profile (name, email address) is linked to your Leave By account via Firebase Authentication.
  • The app requests read-only access to your Google Calendar and imports upcoming events (the next 7 days) so they get leave-by alerts. Nothing is ever written to your calendar.
  • Google Calendar data is used only inside the app for this purpose and is stored alongside your other events. Disconnecting stops all further calendar access; you can also revoke the app's access anytime at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Location

  • Location is requested only while using the app, never in the background, and only when you have back-to-back events — so the app can recalculate travel time from where you actually are, instead of your previous event's address.
  • Your position is used for that calculation and is not stored — not on your device beyond the moment of use, and not in your account.

Route and address processing (Mapbox)

To compute real travel times and turn typed addresses into map coordinates, the app sends the minimum needed — origin/destination coordinates or the address text — from our server to Mapbox, a mapping provider. No event titles, calendar contents, or account details are sent to Mapbox. Mapbox's privacy policy: mapbox.com/legal/privacy.

Service providers

Two third parties process data to make the app work, and only for that purpose:

  • Google Firebase (Firestore database, Authentication, Cloud Functions, Cloud Messaging) — stores your account data and delivers notifications. firebase.google.com/support/privacy
  • Mapbox (routing and geocoding) — receives coordinates/addresses as described above.

We do not use analytics or advertising SDKs, and we do not sell or share your data with anyone else.

Data deletion

  • Events: deleting an event in the app deletes it from your cloud account immediately.
  • Everything: Settings → Account → Delete account permanently removes your account and all server-side data (events, alert schedules, push tokens). You can also email leaveby@spikewave.tech and we'll remove it for you.
  • Uninstalling the app removes all on-device data, but your cloud account persists until deleted as above — that's what lets you reinstall without losing your events.

Children's privacy

Leave By is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect information from anyone under 13 (or the relevant age of digital consent in your region).

Changes to this policy

We'll update this page whenever what the app collects changes, and note the revision date at the top.
