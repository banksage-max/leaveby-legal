Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 17, 2026

Leave By ("the app," "we," "our") is a personal travel-time alert app. This policy explains what information the app handles and how, as of this build.

Summary: Leave By currently stores all of your data locally on your device. The app does not operate any servers, does not collect analytics, and does not share your information with anyone.

Information the app stores

  • Event details you enter: titles, locations (as free text you type), start/end times, event type, and transport mode.
  • App preferences: your default buffer time, and which transport modes and alert stages are enabled.

All of the above is stored only in your device's local app storage (not a cloud account). It is never transmitted anywhere, because the app does not currently connect to the internet to do so.

Information the app does not currently collect

  • No name, email, or account information — the app does not require sign-in.
  • No location access — the app does not request or use location permissions.
  • No calendar access.
  • No analytics, advertising, or tracking of any kind.

Future changes

Leave By is under active development. Planned features will each involve new data handling, and this policy will be updated — with the corresponding permission requested only at the time you use that feature, not before:

  • Calendar sync (Google Calendar, Apple Calendar) — will request read access to the calendars you choose to connect, to import events.
  • Live travel-time routing — will send the origin/destination of an event to Mapbox to compute a route; no other event details leave your device for this.
  • Location — will request "while using the app" location access only, to recalculate a chained event's route after your previous event ends. Never "Always"/background access.
  • Push notifications and account sync — will introduce a cloud backend storing your events so alerts can be delivered even when the app is closed. At that point this policy will explain what's stored server-side and how to delete it.

Data deletion

All data currently lives only on your device. Uninstalling the app deletes all of it. Once cloud sync exists, this section will add in-app and account-level deletion instructions.

Children's privacy

Leave By is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect information from anyone under 13 (or the relevant age of digital consent in your region).

Changes to this policy

We'll update this page whenever what the app collects changes, and note the revision date at the top.
